Sitebulb Logs. Sitebulb has a range of 'global settings' which act as the default settings for every new audit you start. From the top navigation menu, choose the 'Settings' option. From here, the 'Logs' tab allows you to see your system setting and also view all the log files that Sitebulb has generated for any fatal errors that may have occurred during your crawls. These files are really useful when submitting support tickets as they can tell us a lot about what's going on with Sitebulb.
