Filter Hints lists. Got a mountain load of hints and not sure how to whittle them down? We got filters for that! Navigate to your Hints list Open a crawl and jump into your hints, in this example we are going to All Hints in the top right menu, or at the top of the Audits overview. Filter by Hint priority and type Click any of the colorful priority labels to isolate hints by priority. The additional white tags also allow you to filter hints by type - Issues, Opportunities, Ignored Hints, etc. Search Hints On the All Hints page, Sitebulb also allows you to search for specific hints within your list using the search bar. Organize by Importnace or Report The tabs directly under the summary card, allow you to view your hints organized by Importance and Report, just click on each tab to reorder hints respectively. See Changed Hints The changed hints tab isolates hints where metrics have changed since the last website audit. It is particularly useful if you have implemented changes to your pages since you last audited the website.
