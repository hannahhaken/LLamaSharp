Switch Sitebulb into night mode. By default, Sitebulb is set to white, with a nice, bright, readable interface - which is perfect for daytime working in natural light. However, when working at night or in low-light, this brightness can punish the eyes. To alleviate such issues, you can switch Sitebulb into 'night mode', by clicking the 'moon' icon in the top right.
