How to start your free Sitebulb trial. Sitebulb includes a free, fully featured trial for every new user. The 14-day trial gives you access to all of the features available on our Sitebulb Pro plan. Step 1: Download and install the Sitebulb software To get started, you will need to download and install the desktop software. Download Sitebulb from here Step 1: Create your Sitebulb account When you run the software for the first time, you'll be presented with a 'Sign in' page. Select the 'Create a new free account' radio button, fill in your details, and register. Step 3: Verify your account & set your password Once you hit Register , a message will display asking you to verify your account. Check your inbox for the verification email, and follow the link to set your password and verify your Sitebulb account. Step 4: Sign in to Sitebulb through the software Once you've created your Sitebulb account, go back to the software and sign in using your email address and the password you just set. Step 3: Start your free trial An overlay message will pop up, telling you that you have access to a free trial. Click Start Free Trial to activate your free Trial license.
