Pause and resume Audits. If you are partway through an audit, Sitebulb allows you to pause and then resume whenever it is convenient. You may want to do this if you need to shut down your computer in the middle of an audit, for example. Pause the Audit While your audit is buzzing along crawling and processing data, navigate to the Audit progress screen and click the Pause button. This will pause your audit you can now go away a have a cup of tea. View Paused Audits All your paused Audits can be found under the adequately named 'Paused' audits tab from the main Projects dashboard. Resume Audits Once you need to start your crawl, click the View Progress button. You'll be directed back to the Audit progress screen, where you'll find the Resume button. Click the Resume button and Sitebulb will continue from where it left off.
